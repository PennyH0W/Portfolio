My Journey

My interest in web development began early in my career after earning my Webmaster Certificate from St. Petersburg College. During that time, I designed and built websites for a variety of clients, working across both front-end and back-end components. These early projects laid the foundation for my appreciation of thoughtful design, functionality, and problem-solving.

My professional path later led me into healthcare, where I focused on helping individuals regain function and independence. Although my role shifted, my connection to technology never faded. Working in healthcare strengthened my attention to usability, accessibility, and real-world impact—perspectives that continue to shape how I approach technical work today.

In recent years, my work has evolved beyond traditional web development into AI-assisted workflows, model evaluation, and responsible use of AI in real-world systems. I continue to build on my foundation in web development and programming, applying those skills to understand, test, and integrate modern AI technologies in ways that are practical, secure, and human-centered.
